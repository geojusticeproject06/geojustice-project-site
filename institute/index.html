INTERNATIONAL EQUITY GIS

Equity mapping doesn't stop at Atlanta's city limits.

Alongside our Atlanta education programs, The GeoJustice Project's analytical work extends the Institute's mission internationally — putting the same land-equity, community-first methodology to work wherever the data and the need line up.

ANDROS ISLAND, THE BAHAMAS

A nine-year land cover and forest-change baseline for the Caribbean's largest untouched wilderness

The GeoJustice Project built a satellite-based land cover record (2017–2025) and a 24-year forest history extension (2000–2024) for Andros Island — tracking water, forest, wetland, and developed land over time to support local, sustainable land-management and habitat-protection decisions, in the same spirit as our Atlanta parks and equity work.

2017–2025Annual satellite land cover record
-16.6%Forest extent lost since 2000 (Hansen record)
13,761 km²Island area of interest monitored

Findings are mixed and reported as such: near-term satellite data shows tree canopy share holding steady-to-rising since 2017, while the longer 24-year record shows a real net decline since 2000 — concentrated in the last three years on record. Full methodology, sources, and deliverables are documented in the project's Data Sources and Workflow Register.
