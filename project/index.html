01 / PLATFORM

One engine. Five steps from raw parcel data to an auditable decision.

The Decision Platform runs on ArcGIS Experience Builder, Pro, and Enterprise, with the scoring engine hosted on Azure and Google Cloud. This is the pipeline every site record moves through.

01
Ingest
Parcel, census, and agency data pulled into a common schema.
02
Score
Need Engine and Suitability Score run against every site.
03
Rank
Sites ranked under a weighting your team controls and can see.
04
Decide
Staff selects, overrides, or defers — every action is logged.
05
Audit
The Decision Ledger produces the record for council, press, or funders.
02 / MODULES

What's actually inside the platform.

Six modules, built specifically for public land and parks work — not a generic BI dashboard pointed at a shapefile.

Equity Need Engine
Produces the PROE Need Score from four weighted inputs — Physical Conditions, Level of Service, Community Need, Community Perceptions — plus a displacement-risk flag on every parcel.EDT / PROE
Site Suitability Scoring
Park Suitability Score built on Network Analyst, Location-Allocation, and Suitability Modeler, aligned to TPL ParkScore methodology so results hold up against the national benchmark.
Land & Forest Risk
Canopy loss, fire ecology exposure, and land-condition risk layered onto the same parcel record used for siting.
Scenario Studio
The live re-ranking tool in the hero panel above — adjust weighting, compare outcomes, before a decision is final.
Decision Ledger
An immutable audit trail: what was scored, who decided, when, and under what weighting. Built for public accountability, not just internal use.
Reporting & StoryMaps
Council-ready and funder-ready output generated directly from platform data — no manual rebuild in a separate deck.
